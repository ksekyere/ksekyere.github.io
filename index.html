I'm Kofi Forkuo-Sekyere, a quantitative social researcher trained in economics and public policy.

I research the conditions behind decisions: how a country's institutions, trade, health and environment are changing, what the law and the data actually say, and where the risks and openings are. My work is built for people who need that clarity, including founders, investors, attorneys, researchers and economists.

A finding only matters if it reaches people, and what reaches them is often an image, a voice or a sound, not just a number. That's why I also make video and sound pieces, for work meant to outlast a news cycle.
