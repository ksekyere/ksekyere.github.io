I'm Kofi Forkuo-Sekyere, a quantitative social researcher drawn to the research behind documentary, film and other long-form work.

A finding only matters if it reaches people, and what reaches them is often an image, a voice or a sound, not just a number.

I'm most drawn to work meant to last longer than a campaign: documentaries, films, series and long-form projects about family economics, justice and the policies that shape people's lives, at home and abroad. I'm always glad to hear from people making that kind of work.
